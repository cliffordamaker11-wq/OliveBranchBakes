# Phase 3A video demonstration (3–5 minutes)

1. **Introduction (20–30 sec):** Say this is the Olive Branch Bakes Phase 3A checkpoint and identify the models, data persistence, Variant Card, and hand-calculated pricing test plan.
2. **Models (30–45 sec):** Open `Models/BakeryProduct.cs` and `Models/ProductVariant.cs`, then briefly show Customer, Order, and OrderItem.
3. **Seed data and persistence (45–60 sec):** Run the app. Show the seeded product list. Click **Save data**, close the app, open it again, and click **Reload saved data**. Show the product/customer counts and explain where the JSON file is stored.
4. **Variant Card (30–45 sec):** Select multiple products and show their description, base price, and variants/adjustments.
5. **Hand calculations (45–60 sec):** Open `docs/PRICING_RULES_AND_TESTS.md`. Explain the below-threshold, exactly-at-threshold, and one-past-limit cases and show the arithmetic. State that the pricing engine has intentionally not been added until rules are confirmed.
6. **Git history (20–30 sec):** Show the repository commit list and the release tag. Use your actual repository history; do not fabricate dates or commits.

Keep the recording between 3 and 5 minutes. Speak clearly and show the app and relevant files.
